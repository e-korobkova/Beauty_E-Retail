## Executive Summary
An end-to-end customer analytics project designed for a UK-based e-commerce beauty & skincare retailer. Using transactional data from 1,500+ UK customers (£), this project applies **RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) Segmentation** to identify high-value customer groups, detect churn risks, and deliver actionable marketing strategies to optimize Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) and Retention.

---

## Business Problem
The retailer experienced high customer acquisition costs (CAC) alongside a drop in repeat purchases. Marketing campaigns were blast-sent to the entire email database without personalization, leading to low open rates and missed revenue opportunities.

**Key Objectives:**
1. **Segment Customers:** Quantify customer loyalty and spending patterns using RFM scoring.
2. **Reduce Churn:** Identify "At-Risk" and "Hibernating" buyers before they churn completely.
3. **Maximize ROI:** Provide targeted commercial recommendations with estimated ROI impact in GBP (£).
